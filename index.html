Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have a question before booking your free 15-minute call? Here are answers to common questions.

Where do in-person lessons take place in Poznań?

In-person lessons can be held at comfortable, quiet cafés or coworking spots around central Poznań (such as near the Stary Rynek, Jeżyce, or Wilda), or at an agreed quiet public location that fits your schedule. Online lessons are conducted seamlessly via Google Meet or Zoom.

I feel nervous speaking English. Can I still join?

Absolutely! My teaching philosophy puts a strong emphasis on play, curiosity, and zero judgment. You do not need to stress about making mistakes or whether every sentence sounds grammatically perfect. Our lessons are a comfortable sandbox to gain confidence at your own pace.

How does the Spaced Repetition Software (SRS) work?

Spaced repetition is an evidence-based learning technique where new vocabulary, phrasing, and idioms are reviewed at increasing intervals over time. After our lessons, you will receive customized vocabulary cards that you can easily review on your phone in just a few minutes a day, ensuring you never forget what we covered.

What happens during the free 15-minute intro call?

It's a relaxed, no-pressure chat! We will get to know each other, discuss your English learning history and goals, test your current conversational level, align on schedules and meeting preferences (online or in Poznań), and outline a tailored roadmap.

Do I need to purchase any textbooks?

No! You don't need to buy any heavy textbooks. All exercises, reading materials, audio recordings, and study decks are custom-crafted and provided directly inside your shared Google Drive hub.
